You've coached this team for a few weeks. A parent asks "is my kid
getting fair game time?". Answer them with Siren.

1. Find the season's stats: game time per kid, positions played.
2. Who has played least? Most? By how much?
3. Find last week's game summary. Can you share it with parents?
4. Check one kid's time in last week's game matches the season total
   from that game.
5. Find whether the app is doing anything to even it out next game.

Report, most important first and saying where: any number that didn't
add up, anything you couldn't find, and whether you could answer the
parent confidently.
