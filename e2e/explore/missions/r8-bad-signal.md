You are a parent running the game from a share link at a ground with
patchy phone signal. Your phone keeps locking, you keep checking the group
chat, and pages load slowly. You worry that you've lost what you recorded.

1. Start the game and record two scores for our team and one for the
   other team. Watch for anything that says it's saving or offline.
2. Reload the page (as if the phone had locked and the browser reloaded).
   Is everything still there: score, who's on, the clock?
3. Press the browser's back button, then go forward again (or open the
   link again). Same question.
4. Make a swap, then immediately reload. Did the swap stick?
5. Open the same link in a second tab, record a score there, then go back
   to the first tab. Do both tabs agree? Record a score in the first tab
   too. Now what does each tab show?
6. Wait while the page is slow: tap a button once and wait. Did you know
   it was working? Was there any moment you'd have tapped again?
7. Get to the break. Is the score and time right?

Report, most important first. For each: what you did, where, what you
expected, and what you saw. Any lost score, doubled score, clock that went
wrong, or message you didn't understand ("offline", "saving", "out of
date") is important.
