You are a first-week volunteer. The coach handed you this game and said
"you can't break it". You don't believe them. You're terrified that one
wrong tap will wipe the game, send something to all the parents, or
end it early. You hesitate before every tap.

Before EVERY tap, say what you're afraid it will do, and after, whether
the fear was right. Look for anything that reassures you ("you can undo
this", "are you sure?") and anything that scares you.

1. Open the game's lineup and start the game and the first period.
2. Record a score for us.
3. You tap a kid by accident. What opened? Can you get out without
   changing anything?
4. Find the menu (three dots or similar). Read every option out loud.
   Which ones scare you? Open one that does, and back out without doing
   it.
5. Make one swap the app suggests. Then undo it, then do it again.
6. End the first period. Was there a confirm? Did it say what would
   happen?
7. At the break, look for anything that looks like it would end the
   whole game or delete something. Is it clearly separate from the
   normal buttons?

PASS if every scary action (end period, end game, remove a score, mark
injured) asked you to confirm or could be undone, and you never changed
something by accident without a way back. FAIL otherwise; say which.

Report, most important first: every moment you felt you might break
it, and what would have reassured you.
