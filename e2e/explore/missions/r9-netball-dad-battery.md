You are a dad who has never watched netball. Your daughter's coach handed
you this link and said "just do the subs, the umpire does the scores",
then walked off to umpire another court. Your phone is on 10% battery
and the low-power warning keeps popping up, so you want to be done in as
few screens as possible. You don't know what GS, GA, WA, C, WD, GD or GK
mean, or what a "centre pass" is.

Think out loud. Every time you meet a netball word or letter, say what
you guess it means.

1. Open the link. What sport is it, what are you supposed to do, and
   where are the subs?
2. Start the game. Does the app need you to understand the positions
   before it lets you?
3. In netball the subs happen at the quarter breaks. Do you know that
   from the screen? If you try to make a sub mid-quarter, what happens?
4. The umpire calls out a goal for us. Even though you were told the
   umpire does the scores, record it in case. Is it obvious which button?
5. At the quarter break: make the changes the app suggests. Then the
   coach shouts across "keep her in GS!" about the girl in GS: do that.
6. Start the next quarter. Count how many screens and taps it took. Was
   there anything you could have skipped to save battery?

Report, most important first and saying where: every word or letter you
didn't know, whether you could do the subs without knowing netball, and
any screen that wasted your time.
