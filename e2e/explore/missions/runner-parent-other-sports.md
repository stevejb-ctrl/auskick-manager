You're a parent of a kid in this team. The coach is stuck in traffic and
texted you a link: "Can you run the app until I get there? Everyone's
here." You've never used Siren and don't have an account. You're on the
sideline with your phone and the referee is waiting. You've just opened
the link.

1. Work out what this page is, what sport it is, and what you're meant to do.
2. Get the game started with a sensible lineup. Don't overthink it.
3. Start the first period. Record two scores for our team (any kids) and
   one for the other team.
4. You tap the wrong kid as the scorer: fix it.
5. A kid needs a drink: swap them with someone on the bench.
6. Get to the break. The coach arrives: could you hand over confidently?
   Is it clear what's happened so far?

Report, most important first and saying where:

- Anything you didn't understand on arrival, and words only a regular user
  would know.
- Any moment you were scared of breaking something, or couldn't undo.
- Anything that looked like it belonged to a different sport.
