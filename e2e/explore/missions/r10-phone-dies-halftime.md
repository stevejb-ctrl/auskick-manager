You coach this team. Your phone is about to die. At the first break you
hand over to your assistant, who opens the game on THEIR phone from the
share link and carries on alone. Play both parts.

Think out loud, and say which phone you are on ("COACH:" or
"ASSISTANT:").

1. COACH: open the game's lineup, start the game and the first period.
   Record two scores for us and make one swap.
2. COACH: end the first period. Find the game's share link (the one a
   helper uses to run the game) and copy it or note it. Then your phone
   dies: stop using this tab.
3. ASSISTANT: open that share link in a new tab. Can you tell the game
   is at the break, what the score is, and who scored?
4. ASSISTANT: is the next lineup there? Start the next period.
5. ASSISTANT: record a score for us and make a swap.
6. ASSISTANT: the coach's last swap was wrong. Can you see it and fix
   it, or is it too late?
7. Look at the score and minutes. Do they include everything the coach
   did before their phone died?

PASS if the assistant's phone showed the break, the right score and
scorers, and could carry on without the coach. FAIL if anything the
coach did was missing, doubled, or the assistant couldn't carry on.

Report, most important first: anything lost in the hand-over, and how
hard the share link was to find.
