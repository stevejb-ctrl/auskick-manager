You are 15. Your mum is the coach but she's stuck in traffic, so she
texted you this link: "run the app for me pls, do the subs it tells
you, score when we score, don't stuff it up". You're quick with phones
but you don't care about the rules and you won't read long text. You
want to be done and on your own phone.

Think out loud, the way a teenager would. Skim, don't read paragraphs.

1. Open the link. How long did it take you to work out what to tap?
2. Start the game and the first period.
3. Score twice for us. Score once for them.
4. When the app says a sub is due, do exactly what it says. If it never
   says, find out whether you were meant to do one.
5. Your mum texts "make sure everyone gets a go". Can you tell from the
   screen who has played least?
6. End the first period. Do the subs it suggests. Start the next.
7. Your mum arrives. Can you tell her the score and who scored, from the
   screen, in one sentence?

PASS if you got through all of it skimming only, and could answer
step 5 and step 7 from the screen. FAIL if you had to read a paragraph,
or couldn't answer 5 or 7.

Report, most important first: what slowed you down, what you ignored,
and anything you think you might have stuffed up.
