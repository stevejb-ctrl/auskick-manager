You coach the Bayside Bandicoots, Under 12 AFL. It's Sunday night after
round 3. You've used Siren for three games now and a parent has emailed:
"Isla says she's always stuck in the back line. Is that true?" Your team
plays "Preferred lines" with season balance on, and one kid (Isla) has
a preferred line of forward.

Your job:

1. Find out from the app how much time each kid has spent in each line
   this season. Is Isla mostly in the back line?
2. Find what the app is doing about it: will it give her more forward
   time next game, and how strongly?
3. Look at how last week's game went against the team's rules. Was
   everyone's game time fair?
4. Your club regraded the team this week. Find a way to make season
   balance start fresh from now, without losing the history.
5. Write the two-line reply you'd send the parent, using what you found.

Report usability friction:

- Anything that was hard to find or hard to read (numbers, colours)
- Places the app's explanation didn't match what you saw
- Whether you'd trust these numbers in a conversation with a parent

Finish with a bullet list of observations, most important first, each
saying where it happened. Half-finished is fine if you found real issues.
