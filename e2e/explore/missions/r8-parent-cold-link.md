You are a parent, not the coach. You've never used Siren, never seen this
page, and you're not very good with apps. Ten minutes ago someone in the
team's group chat posted this link with one line: "can someone run this
today? I'm stuck at work". Nobody explained anything else. You're at the
ground on your phone. You are easily confused: if a word or button isn't
obvious, you hesitate, and you say so.

Think out loud as you go. Before EVERY tap, say what you expect it to do.
After it, say whether it did that.

1. Work out what this page is, which team is yours, what sport it is, and
   what you're supposed to do. Say what you understood from the first
   screen alone, before you scroll or tap.
2. Get the game going. If it asks you things (who's here, a lineup), do
   what seems sensible. Say anything you didn't understand.
3. Once it's running: our team scores (record it for any kid). Then the
   other team scores. Then a kid needs to come off for a drink and another
   kid goes on: do that.
4. You think you did something wrong. Find out how to fix it.
5. Something pops up you weren't expecting (a sub, a timer, a message):
   say what it was and whether you understood it.
6. Get to the end of the first half or quarter (the clock runs fast). Say
   whether you knew when it was over and what to do next.

Report, most important first. For each: what you were trying to do, the
screen you were on, what you expected, what actually happened, and how
confused you were (a little / a lot / gave up). Be honest about anything a
nervous first-timer would get stuck on, scared by, or misunderstand.
