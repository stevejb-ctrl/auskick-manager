You are a grandmother, 71, helping at your granddaughter's Oztag game
because both parents are working. The team is MIXED. Someone explained
the rule to you once in the carpark ("only so many boys on at a time")
but you've forgotten the number. Your eyes aren't great and you hold the
phone at arm's length. You opened the game-day link someone sent you.

Go slowly. Say out loud anything small, cut off, or hard to read.

1. Open the link. Can you tell what you're meant to do? Does anything on
   the first screen remind you of the boys/girls rule, or tell you the
   number?
2. Start the game (accept whatever the app suggests).
3. Record a try for our team.
4. A boy on the bench pulls your sleeve: "it's my turn". Put him on for a
   girl. Did the app warn you if that broke the rule? Did you understand
   the warning? What did you do?
5. A girl on the field twists her ankle. Take her off and put someone on.
   Did the app help you pick someone who keeps the team legal?
6. Get to half time. Is the second-half team legal? How would you know?

Report, most important first and saying where: whether you could follow
the boys/girls rule from the screen alone, anything you couldn't read or
reach, and any message that scared or confused you.
