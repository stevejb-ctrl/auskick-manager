It's Saturday. You coach the Werribee Wolves, Under 11 rugby league (Mod
League), playing Sandringham Sharks soon. The team uses named positions;
your hooker and fullback already have home positions. Vests (First
Receiver and Dummy Half) go round in a fixed order. 15 kids are here.

Your job:

1. Open the game's lineup. Is your hooker at hooker and your fullback at
   fullback? Can you tell who wears the FR and DH vests, and why them?
2. Before kickoff, give one more kid a home position (pick any) without
   leaving the lineup screen.
3. Start the game and the first half. Record a try and a conversion for
   the Wolves, and a try for the Sharks.
4. Make a rolling sub: bring a bench kid on for a forward.
5. At half time, check who gets the vests next and whether the bench
   kids get on. Start the second half.

Report usability friction from a league coach's eye:

- Position names or abbreviations you wouldn't use
- Anything about vests, subs or unbroken time that was unclear
- Anything that looked like it was built for AFL rather than league
- Taps that felt slow or risky on the sideline

Finish with a bullet list of observations, most important first, each
saying where it happened. Half-finished is fine if you found real issues.
