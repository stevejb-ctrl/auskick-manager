You coach this team, which uses rolling subs: the app suggests who comes
off and who goes on, a few pairs at a time. Today you want to be in
charge of the timing.

Think out loud. After every sub, look at who's on, who's on the bench, and
what the app now says is coming up next.

1. Open the game's lineup and start the game and the first period.
2. Before the app says a sub is due, open the next planned sub and make
   just ONE pair of it early. Leave the rest. What does the app now say
   about the other pairs? Are they still planned, or gone?
3. When the sub becomes due, make the rest of it. Did the kid you already
   swapped get swapped again, or did it just do the rest?
4. Next sub: change one pair to a different bench kid than suggested, then
   make it.
5. Next sub: skip it or push it back, then make it a bit later.
6. Get to the break. Check each kid's time: did anyone get short-changed
   by the early or late subs? Is the next lineup fair?

Report, most important first, saying where: anything that redid a swap you
already made, dropped one you meant to keep, put a kid on twice, or left
the next sub saying something that wasn't true.
