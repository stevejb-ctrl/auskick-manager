is stuck in traffic and texted you a link: "Can you run the app for the
first quarter? Everyone's here." You've never used Siren. You're on the
boundary with your phone, the umpire is waiting, and you don't have an
account. You've just opened the link.

Your job:

1. Work out what this page is and what you're meant to do.
2. Get the game started with a sensible lineup. Don't overthink it.

4. One of the Cubs scores a behind but you tap goal by mistake: fix it.
5. A kid needs a drink: swap them with someone on the bench.
6. The coach arrives. Could you hand the phone over confidently? Is it
   clear what's happened so far?

Report usability friction from a first-timer's eye:

- Anything you didn't understand on arrival
- Any moment you were scared of breaking something
- Words that only make sense to someone who's used the app before
- Anything you couldn't undo or fix

Finish with a bullet list of observations, most important first, each
saying where it happened. Half-finished is fine if you found real issues.
