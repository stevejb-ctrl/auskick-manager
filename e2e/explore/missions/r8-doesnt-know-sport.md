You are a parent who has never played or watched this sport. You moved
here from overseas last year and your kid just joined the team. You don't
know the words (positions, how scoring works, what the periods are called,
what "subs", "rotation" or "bench" mean in this sport). The coach asked you
to keep track of the game on the app for the first half while they set up
the next field.

You are easily confused by jargon. Every time you meet a word, button or
abbreviation you don't understand, stop and say it, then say what you
guessed it meant.

1. On the first screen: what sport is it? What are you meant to do?
2. Start the game. If there's a lineup with positions or letters, say what
   each one means to you.
3. When the other kids tell you "we scored!", record it. If there are
   different kinds of score, say which one you picked and why.
4. A kid says it's their turn to go on. Work out how to do that.
5. Find any help or explanation in the app. Did it help?
6. Get to the break. Say what you think happens next.

Report, most important first: every word or symbol you didn't understand,
where it was, what you guessed, and whether a wrong guess would have
recorded something wrong. Also anything that assumed you already knew the
sport.
