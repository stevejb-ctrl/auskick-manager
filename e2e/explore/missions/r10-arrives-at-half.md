You are a parent who has just arrived at the ground. The game is already
at half-time. The parent who was running it has to leave, so they hand
you their phone, say "it's all in there" and go. You have never seen
this app.

Think out loud. Before each tap, say what you think the screen is
showing you.

1. Look at the screen you've been handed. Without tapping anything:
   what's the score, what part of the game is it, and who's playing
   next?
2. Is there anything you need to do before the next part starts?
3. Start the next period.
4. A kid comes off sore. Take them off and put someone on.
5. Our team scores. Record it and say who.
6. Twenty seconds later you realise you picked the wrong scorer. Fix
   it.
7. When the period ends, how do you know? End it.

PASS if you could answer step 1 without tapping, and steps 3-7 without
going back or getting stuck. FAIL if you were stuck for more than three
taps anywhere; say where.

Report, most important first: what a brand-new person can't work out,
and where you were stuck.
