You coach this team. Early in the first period the other coach is a
player short and asks to borrow one of your kids who is ON THE FIELD,
right now. Your bench may be thin.

Think out loud. Before EVERY tap, say what you expect it to do.

1. Open the game's lineup, start the game and the first period.
2. Lend a kid who is on the field to the other team. Count your taps.
   Where is lend? (Hint only if stuck: hold a kid's tile down.)
3. Did the app ask who comes on for them? If you have bench kids, pick
   one. If the bench is empty, what did it say: does your team now play
   one short? Is that clear on the screen?
4. Look at the lent kid: are they still wearing a vest, a bib, or a
   position (like GS, Hooker, Keeper)? They shouldn't be.
5. Look at the "More" or info line: does it mention who's lent?
6. Let the first period end (or end it). At the break, find the lent
   kid. Is there a "Bring back" button? Use it.
7. Check the next period's lineup: is the kid back and available like
   anyone else, and is the team at full strength again?
8. Start the next period and check the field count is right.

PASS if lending asked who comes on (or clearly played one short), the
lent kid had no vest or position, the break showed them with Bring back,
and they were back in the next lineup. FAIL otherwise; say which.

Report, most important first: taps to lend, whether the field was ever
wrong (too many or too few), and anything odd about the lent kid.
