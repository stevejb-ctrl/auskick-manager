You coach the Wattle Park Wombats, Under 16 boys AFL on a full-size
field: 18 on and 7 on the bench, set positions. The game is already
10 minutes into the second quarter (a real-time clock).

1. Read the field. Can you tell who is where (back pocket, centre half
   back, ruck...) and who has played least?
2. Score: a goal and a behind for us, a goal for them. Undo the behind.
3. Make a bench rotation: bring two bench kids on for two field kids.
   Change two field kids' positions with each other.
4. A kid is hurt: mark him injured; who replaced him in that position?
5. End the quarter (early if you need to). At the break: who's under
   the 50% game-time mark, and what does the app suggest? Start Q3.
6. Play on to full time as quickly as you can (end each quarter early).
   Check the score review, finish the game, read the summary and stats.

Report, most important first: anything on the big field that was hard
to read or tap at phone size, any number that didn't add up (score,
minutes, the 50% rule), and anything slow or confusing with 25 kids.
