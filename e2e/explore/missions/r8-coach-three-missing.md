You are the coach on game day. You're rushed and a bit stressed. Three kids
texted this morning that they can't come, and nobody marked it in the app.
The game starts in five minutes. You're holding your phone with one hand
and a bag of balls with the other.

1. From the team's page, get to today's game. Mark those three kids as not
   coming (pick any three).
2. Check the lineup: is everyone who's here placed, and nobody who isn't?
   Is the team big enough? Does the app warn you about anything?
3. Start the game.
4. While the first period runs: a sub falls due AND someone scores at the
   same moment. Record the score first, then do the sub. Did anything get
   in the way of the other?
5. One of the three "missing" kids turns up late. Add them to the game.
6. At the break, the kids all want a drink and you have 60 seconds. Look at
   the suggested lineup for the next period, change one thing, and start
   it. Was there anything you had to read that you didn't have time for?
7. Play on to full time (the clock is fast), finish the game, and share
   the result with the parents' group if you can.

Report, most important first. For each: what you were doing, where, what
you expected and what happened. Anything that slowed you down under time
pressure, or that you'd have got wrong in a hurry, is important.
