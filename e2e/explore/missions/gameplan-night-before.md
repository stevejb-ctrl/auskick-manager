You coach the Kew Kookaburras, an Under 13 AFL team that plays set
positions. It's Friday night and tomorrow's game against Sandringham
Sharks is at 4pm. Four kids already have home positions (full forward,
full back, centre, ruck). You like to post the rotation in the parents'
group chat the night before so families know who starts where.

Your job:

1. From the app, find the game and plan the rotation for the whole game.
   Does the plan put the four kids in their home positions?
2. Make one change you care about (say, a kid who missed last week
   starts on the field). Get the plan as text you could paste into the
   group chat.
3. Now imagine it's tomorrow at the ground. Open the game's lineup. Does
   it match what you planned last night? If not, can you make the plan
   and the lineup agree without redoing everything?
4. Using the plan, set the first quarter's lineup, then check the lineup
   screen shows it.

Report usability friction:

- Anything that made the plan and the lineup feel like two different
  things
- Anything you planned that you couldn't carry over to game day
- Whether the text you'd paste into the chat reads well to parents

Finish with a bullet list of observations, most important first, each
saying where it happened. Half-finished is fine if you found real issues.
