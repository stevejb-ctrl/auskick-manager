You coach a MIXED touch team: 6 on the field at once, and the rule is no
more than 3 boys on at any time (and at least one girl). One kid wasn't
marked available: they're running late. It's a chaotic morning and you
have to keep the app honest while things change fast. After EVERY step,
count the boys and girls on the field yourself, and note anything odd: a
kid in two places, an injured kid on the field or in a suggestion, a mix
that breaks the rule without the app saying so, a count that doesn't add
up.

Do these in order (it's fine if a step needs tapping a kid, or a menu):

1. Open the game's lineup, check the mix, and start the game and the first
   half.
2. Record a try for us, and one for the other team.
3. Mid-half: you want to bring a boy on for a girl, so that 4 boys would
   be on. Try it. What does the app do? Can you do it anyway? If it lets
   you, can you tell the mix is now wrong?
4. A girl on the field is hurt: mark her injured. Who replaces her? Is the
   mix still legal? Did the app pick for you or ask?
5. The late kid arrives: add them.
6. The suggested sub comes up: make only one pair of it, leave the rest
   for later, and check the mix again.
7. Get to half time. Check the injured girl isn't in the second-half
   lineup, the mix of the second-half lineup, and start the second half.

Report every inconsistency you saw, most important first, saying exactly
which step and screen it was on. Also say which action was hardest to find.
