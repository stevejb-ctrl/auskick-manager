You coach the Camberwell Comets, Under 13 hockey: 11 a side with a
goalkeeper, four quarters. 15 kids are here for the game against the Box
Hill Bats. Play the WHOLE game in Siren, all four quarters, through to the
finished game. The clock runs fast, so keep moving.

1. Open the game's lineup, check the goalkeeper and the bench, and start
   the game and Q1.
2. In each quarter: record a goal for the Comets (different scorers each
   time) and, in two of the quarters, a goal for the Bats. Undo one goal.
   Make the subs when they're suggested.
3. In Q2 one of your kids is hurt: mark them injured and bring someone on.
   In Q3 they feel better: let them play again.
4. At each break: look at the suggested lineup, change one thing
   yourself, then start the next quarter.
5. After Q4: check the scores quarter by quarter in the review, fix one,
   then finish the game.
6. Look at the finished game page and the team's season stats.

Report, most important first and saying where:

- Anything that didn't add up (scores, quarter-by-quarter totals, game
  time, who played where, the injured kid).
- Anywhere you weren't sure a tap had worked or been saved.
- Anything you couldn't find, or found in a surprising place.
- Whether you'd trust the finished summary enough to send it to parents.
