You coach this team. Halfway through the first period the other coach
waves at you: they're a player short and asks if you can lend them one of
yours, right now, a kid who is ON THE FIELD (the umpire won't wait for
you to pick from the bench). Later they'll give the kid back.

Think out loud. Before EVERY tap, say what you expect it to do.

1. Open the game's lineup and start the game and the first period.
2. Record a score for us.
3. Lend a kid who is on the field to the other team. Time yourself in
   taps: how many taps, and where did you look first? Say each place you
   tried that didn't work.
4. Once lent: is the kid off the field? Did the app ask who comes on for
   them, or leave a gap? Can you fill the gap?
5. Check the lent kid isn't being suggested in any sub or counted as
   playing time.
6. The other coach is done with them before the break: get the kid back
   mid-period. Then at the break, check they're in the next lineup like
   anyone else.

Report, most important first: how hard the lend was to find (taps and
wrong turns), whether the field was left short, and anything that didn't
add up for the lent kid.
