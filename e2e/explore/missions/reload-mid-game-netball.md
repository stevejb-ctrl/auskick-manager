You coach the Hawthorn Hornets, 11 & Under netball. Your phone keeps
locking and you keep switching apps, so Siren reloads on you. You need to
know nothing is lost when that happens.

1. Open the game's lineup and start the game and Q1.
2. Record two goals for the Hornets. Then your GA hurts her ankle: mark
   her injured and bring a bench girl on at GA.
3. **Reload the page** (browser refresh). Check: the score, the clock, who
   is in each position, the bench, the injured girl, each girl's time.
   Anything different from before the reload?
4. Lend a girl on court to the Sharks, and leave her position empty for a
   moment. Reload. Is the position still empty and the girl still lent?
   Then fill the position from the bench.
5. Let Q1 end. At the break, change two positions, then **reload before
   you start Q2**. Are your changes still there? Start Q2.
6. In Q2, check the time bars of the girl who came on at GA in Q1 and the
   injured girl. Do they make sense for how long each was on court?

Report every difference you noticed after a reload, most important first,
saying which step it was.
