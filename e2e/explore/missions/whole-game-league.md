You coach the Werribee Wolves, Under 11 rugby league (junior laws: First
Receiver and Dummy Half vests, a kickoff rotation, conversions). 15 kids
are here for the game against the Sandringham Sharks. Play the WHOLE game
in Siren, both halves, through to the finished game. The clock runs fast,
so keep moving.

1. Open the game's lineup, check the vests and positions, and start the
   game and the first half. Record who kicks off.
2. In each half: record at least two tries for the Wolves (different
   scorers) with a conversion attempt after each, and a try for the
   Sharks. Make the rolling subs when they're suggested.
3. In the first half your First Receiver is hurt: mark them injured, bring
   someone on, and make sure someone wears the FR vest.
4. At half time: check the vests for the second half, change one thing in
   the lineup yourself, and start the second half (with its kickoff).
5. After full time: check the scores in the review, fix one you "got
   wrong", then finish the game.
6. Look at the finished game: the result, tries and conversions per kid,
   each kid's time on the field, and the summary. Copy or share it if you
   can.

Report, most important first and saying where:

- Anything that didn't add up (scores, conversions, vests, kickoffs, time
  on the field, the injured kid).
- Anywhere you weren't sure a tap had worked or been saved.
- Whether you'd trust the finished summary enough to send it to parents.
