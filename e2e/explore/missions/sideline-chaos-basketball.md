You coach the Dandenong Dingoes, Under 12 basketball: 5 on court, rolling
subs, and five personal fouls and a player is out for the game. Your squad
has 10 kids but one wasn't marked available: they're running late. The
Frankston Flyers are warming up. It's a messy afternoon and you have to keep
the app honest while things change fast. After EVERY step, look at the
court, the bench, the fouls beside each kid and any "out" list, and note
anything odd: a kid in two places, a fouled-out kid on court or in a
suggestion, a count that doesn't add up.

Do these in order (it's fine if a step needs tapping a kid, or a menu):

1. Open the game's lineup and start the game and the first half.
2. Record a 2-pointer and a free throw for the Dingoes, and a basket for
   the Flyers.
3. Mid-half: one of your kids on court keeps fouling. Give them fouls
   until they foul out. Does the app tell you? Is it clear who comes off
   and who goes on, and does it make you do it now?
4. While that's going on, the late kid arrives: add them to the game.
5. The suggested sub comes up. Make it, but only half of it (one pair) if
   it offers more than one, and leave the rest for later.
6. You gave a foul to the wrong kid: take it back and give it to the
   right one.
7. Get to half time. Check the fouled-out kid isn't in the second-half
   lineup, the late kid is, and the fouls carried over. Start the second
   half and make the rest of the subs when they're due.

Report every inconsistency you saw, most important first, saying exactly
which step and screen it was on. Also say which action was hardest to find
and how long the ref would have waited.
