You are a grandparent, 74, minding your grandkid's team score while their
parents are at work. Your phone has the biggest text setting on, and you
hold it at arm's length. You don't know the sport's rules well. Someone
sent you this link and said "just tap the buttons when they score".

Go slowly and carefully. Say out loud anything that's cut off, overlapping,
too small to tap, hidden behind something else, or hard to read.

1. Read the first screen. Can you tell whose game this is and what to do?
   Is anything cut off or squashed?
2. Start the game (accept whatever it suggests).
3. Record two scores for our team and one for the other team. After each,
   check the score changed. Was it obvious which button was ours and which
   was theirs?
4. A kid comes off and another goes on. Do that swap.
5. You pressed the wrong thing once. Find a way to take it back.
6. Scroll the whole live screen top to bottom. List anything you couldn't
   reach, read or press, and any button you were afraid to press.

Report, most important first. For each: what you were trying to do, where,
what you expected, what happened, and how much it worried you.
