It's Saturday. You coach the Werribee Wolves, Under 13 rugby league, playing
Sandringham Sharks soon. This is your first season past Mod League: it's
13-a-side on full international rules now, so there are no vests and no
kicking rotation, and penalty goals and field goals count. Your halfback
is your goal-kicker. 17 kids are here.

Your job:

1. Open the game's lineup. Does it look like a 13-a-side team with real
   positions? Is anything left over from the junior game (vests, kicking
   rotations, "unbroken" rules) that shouldn't be there at this age?
2. Start the game and the first half.
3. Record a try for the Wolves, then a conversion by your halfback. Then
   another try and a second conversion by the SAME halfback.
4. Record a penalty goal by the halfback, and a field goal by anyone.
5. Record a penalty goal and a field goal for the Sharks.
6. One of those was a mistake: undo the last score. Check the score is
   right (Wolves 4 + 2 + 4 + 2 + 2 + 1 = 15, Sharks 2 + 1 = 3 before the
   undo).
7. End the first half and check the score at the break.

Report usability friction from a league coach's eye:

- Was it obvious where to record a penalty goal or a field goal?
- Did anything stop the same kicker taking every kick?
- Anything that still looked like junior league or AFL
- Taps that felt slow or risky on the sideline

Finish with a bullet list of observations, most important first, each
saying where it happened. Half-finished is fine if you found real issues.
