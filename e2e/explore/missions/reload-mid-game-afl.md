You coach the Bayside Bandicoots, Under 12 AFL. Your phone keeps locking
and you keep switching to the group chat, so Siren reloads on you. You
need to know nothing is lost when that happens.

1. Open the game's lineup and start the game and Q1.
2. Record a goal and a behind for the Bandicoots, lend a kid on the field
   to the opposition, and make one swap.
3. **Reload the page** (browser refresh). Check: the score, the clock,
   who's on the field and the bench, the lent kid, each kid's time.
   Anything different from before the reload?
4. Mark a kid on the field injured and bring someone on. Then reload
   again and check the same things.
5. Let Q1 end. At the break, change two positions, then **reload before
   you start Q2**. Are your changes still there?
6. Start Q2, record a score, then go back to the team's home screen and
   come back into the game the way a coach would (not with the back
   button). Is everything where you left it?

Report every difference you noticed after a reload or after coming back,
most important first, saying which step it was.
