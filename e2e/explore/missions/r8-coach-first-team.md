You've just been talked into coaching your kid's junior team. You have
never used Siren or any team app. You've signed in for the first time and
landed on the dashboard. You're not technical and you're easily put off.

The team: pick a sport from what Siren offers that isn't Australian
football (say which one you picked), and your kid's age group (Under 10
or Under 11).

1. Set up the team. Say anything you didn't understand on each step, and
   anything you were asked that you didn't know the answer to.
2. Add eight kids to the squad (any names; make one of them "Charlotte
   Rose McKenzie-Whitfield" to see how long names look). Give them
   numbers.
3. Find how the team plays (rotations, positions, game time) and say
   whether you understood the choices well enough to pick.
4. Add Saturday's game against "Eastside".
5. Send the parents a way to say who's coming, if there is one.
6. Find out how you'd get another parent to run the game on the day.

Report, most important first. For each: the step, the screen, what you
expected, what happened, and how stuck you were. Words you didn't know and
choices you couldn't make are as important as anything broken.
