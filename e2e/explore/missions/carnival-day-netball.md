It's carnival day. You coach the Preston Panthers, 11U netball. Your club
plays three games this morning and the rule is that every girl plays
every third (attack, centre, defence) across the day. The team already
has that set up in Siren. You played your first game a couple of hours
ago; the second, against Sandringham Sharks, starts in ten minutes.
10 girls are here.

Your job:

1. Open the second game's lineup. Does anything tell you who still needs
   which third today? Does the suggested lineup give those girls their
   missing third?
2. Ava hasn't been in attack yet today. Make sure she starts in attack
   this game.
3. Start the game and the first quarter. Record a goal.
4. At the quarter break, check the suggestions still respect the
   carnival rule. Start Q2.
5. Imagine a parent asks "has my daughter played every third today?".
   Could you answer from the app?

Report usability friction:

- Whether the carnival rule felt visible or hidden
- Anything that made you doubt the app knew about this morning's game
- Words a netball coach wouldn't use

Finish with a bullet list of observations, most important first, each
saying where it happened. Half-finished is fine if you found real issues.
