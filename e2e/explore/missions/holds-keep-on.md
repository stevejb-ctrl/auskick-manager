It's Saturday. You coach the Bayside Bandicoots, Under 12 AFL, playing
Sandringham Sharks soon. Two things are on your mind:

- Mia is just back from a broken wrist and needs match practice: you
  want her on the field the whole of TODAY's game, but only today.
- Lachlan is your most reliable kid. You want the app to always favour
  giving him more time, EVERY week, as a standing preference.

Your job:

1. Set both of those up before the game starts. Use whatever the app
   offers; notice whether it's clear which option is "today only" and
   which is "every week".
2. Check you can see on the lineup that Mia is staying on.
3. Start the game and the first quarter. Is Mia still marked as staying
   on? Record one goal.
4. At the quarter break, do the suggested changes keep Mia on? Start Q2.
5. Change your mind: let Mia rotate normally from now on.

Report usability friction:

- Any confusion between "this game" and "every game"
- Anything you set that didn't seem to stick, or didn't show anywhere
- Places where the same idea had different names

Finish with a bullet list of observations, most important first, each
saying where it happened. Half-finished is fine if you found real issues.
