You coach Kingsway United, Under 9 football (soccer, MiniRoos), against the
Coogee Crabs: 7 a side with a keeper, two 20-minute halves. 10 kids are
here. MiniRoos keeps no score, but your club tracks goals for the kids, so
scoring is on.

Play the whole game, start to finish, the way you would on a Saturday:

1. Pick the lineup (take the suggestion unless something looks wrong) and
   start the first half.
2. Through the half, make the subs when they're due. Record two goals for
   Kingsway and one for the Crabs as they happen.
3. End the first half. Use the half-time plan for the second half.
4. Through the second half, keep making the subs. Record one more goal for
   each side.
5. End the game, check the score with the other coach, and finish it.
6. Read the summary you'd paste into the parents' group chat. Did every
   kid get a fair go? Who went in goal, and for how long? Then open the
   team's stats.

Report on fairness and finish:

- Did the minutes come out even? Who played most and least, and by how much?
- Did the keeper turns make sense across the two halves?
- Was the summary something you'd send as is?
- Anything that read like another sport

Finish with a bullet list of observations, most important first, each
saying where it happened.
