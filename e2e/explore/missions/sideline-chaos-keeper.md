You coach this team: it plays with a goalkeeper and rolling subs. It's a
chaotic morning and you have to keep the app honest while things change
fast. After EVERY step, look at who is in goal, the field, the bench and
any "injured" list, and note anything odd: no keeper, two keepers, a kid
in two places, an injured kid on the field or in a suggestion, a count
that doesn't add up.

Do these in order (it's fine if a step needs tapping a kid, or a menu):

1. Open the game's lineup, check who's in goal, and start the game and the
   first half.
2. Record a goal for us and one for the other team.
3. Mid-half: your **keeper gets hurt** in a collision. Mark them injured.
   Who goes in goal? Does the app ask, or pick someone? Is the field still
   full?
4. You'd rather a kid from the **bench** goes in goal, not a field kid.
   Put a bench kid in goal.
5. The other team is short: **lend** them one of the kids on the field.
   Was it easy to find? Did anyone come on for them?
6. Make one normal sub, then undo the last goal.
7. Get to half time. Check the injured keeper isn't in the second-half
   lineup, who is in goal for the second half, and that the lent kid can
   come back. Start the second half.

Report every inconsistency you saw, most important first, saying exactly
which step and screen it was on. Also say which action was hardest to find.
