You coach an Under 11 rugby league team (Mod League) and you've just
signed in to Siren for the first time. Your kids are starting to learn
real positions: you want a hooker, props, halves and so on, and you
want the First Receiver and Dummy Half vests shared out in a fixed
order.

Your goal: get the team set up for this week's game.

1. Create a team called "Werribee Wolves", Junior Rugby League, Under 11.
2. When the app asks how your team plays, choose the option with named
   positions. Look at the positions offered. Then find how the vests
   are handed out and choose a fixed order.
3. Add 13 players (make up names, give them jersey numbers).
4. Make one player your hooker (home position), and say another never
   plays in the forwards.
5. Schedule a game against "Melton Mustangs" this Saturday.
6. Open the game-day lineup. Can you tell who's playing where, and who
   wears which vest? Long-press a player and see what you can do.

Report usability friction as you go:

- Position names or abbreviations a league coach wouldn't recognise
- Anything about vests that was unclear
- Settings that were hard to find or that didn't seem to stick
- Anything that looked like it was built for AFL and not league

Finish with a bullet list of observations, most important first, each
saying where it happened. Half-finished is fine if you found real issues.
