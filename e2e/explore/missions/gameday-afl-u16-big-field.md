You coach the Wattle Park Wombats, Under 16 boys AFL: 18 on the field in
set positions and 7 on the bench. You're ten minutes into the second
quarter against the Kew Kookas, the phone is already on the live screen,
and the clock is running at real speed. Your league says every boy has to
play at least half the game.

Your job, as you would on the boundary:

1. Read the field. Can you tell every boy's position at a glance (full
   back, centre half back, wings, full forward...)? Is anyone cut off or
   too small to read?
2. Your centre half back and a half forward should trade spots for the
   rest of the quarter. Change their positions without taking anyone off.
   Did both boys move? If you got it wrong, take it back.
3. Look for any boy who's short of the minimum time. Where does Siren
   tell you, and how many minutes does he still need?
4. Bring one of those boys on for someone who's had plenty of time.
   Check where he ended up and whether the warning changed.
5. Record a goal for your full forward and a behind for someone else.
   Then fix one of them by tapping the score.
6. Open the ⋯ menu. What's in it? Find the game-day link a parent could
   use, but don't send it to anyone.

Report, most important first and saying where:

- Anything that didn't add up (positions, who's on, minutes, the
  minimum-time warning, the score).
- Anywhere you weren't sure a tap had worked, or did something you
  didn't mean.
- Words you'd misread at a glance on a busy boundary.
