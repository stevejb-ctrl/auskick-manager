period is over NOW, but the clock on your phone still has time left. You

to be and why.

2. Record one score for us so the period isn't empty.

4. When you find it: what did the button say ("End Q1", "End 1st half",

   you? Does the line under the score say something like "Time's up:
   end ..."? Tap it and end the period from there.
