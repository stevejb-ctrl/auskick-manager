You coach an 11U netball team and you've just signed in to Siren for the
first time. Your club plays carnival days (three games in one morning)
as well as normal Saturday rounds. Parents are sensitive about
fairness: every girl should get a go in every third, and nobody should
be stuck in one bib all season.

Your goal: set the team up so the app rotates the way you want.

1. Create a team called "Preston Panthers", Junior Netball, 11U.
2. When the app asks how your team plays, read each rotation style it
   offers. Pick the one that best gives every kid a turn at every
   position over the season. Note anything you didn't understand.
3. Find the season balance setting. Do you understand what it does and
   how strong it is?
4. Find a way to make sure every player plays every third across a
   carnival day, and turn it on.
5. Add 10 players (make up names).
6. One girl is your best shooter: give her a home position of GS, but
   make sure she still rotates.
7. Schedule a game against "Reservoir Rockets" this Saturday and open
   its game-day lineup. Does the suggested lineup look fair?

Report usability friction as you go:

- Rotation style names or descriptions that didn't make sense
- Settings you couldn't find, or found in a surprising place
- Anything that contradicted what you'd told the app
- Words a netball coach wouldn't use

Finish with a bullet list of observations, most important first, each
saying where it happened. Half-finished is fine if you found real issues.
