It's Saturday. You coach the Hawthorn Hornets, an 11U netball team. The
team is set up in Siren with 9 girls (so two sit out each quarter) and
it rotates "Position by position". The game against Sandringham Sharks
starts soon. Your co-coach might take over the phone at half time.

Your job:

1. Get to this game's lineup and look at the suggested starting seven.
   Is it clear who's sitting out and why?
2. Your GS is in form: make sure she stays at GS at the next break.
3. Start the game. Record two goals for your team and one for the
   opposition, then undo the opposition goal.
4. At the quarter break, check the suggested changes. Is GS still your
   shooter? Do the two girls who sat out get on? Start Q2.
5. Imagine you hand the phone to your co-coach: would they understand
   what's going on from the screen alone?

Report usability friction:

- Anything that was hard to read or tap on a phone
- Places where you weren't sure the app remembered what you'd asked
- Rotation suggestions that seemed unfair, with no explanation
- Words a netball coach wouldn't use

Finish with a bullet list of observations, most important first, each
saying where it happened. Half-finished is fine if you found real issues.
