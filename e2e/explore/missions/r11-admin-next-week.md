You coach this team. The next game is coming up. Tonight you sort out
who's coming.

1. Find the next game and who's said they're available.
2. Find a way to ask the parents who's coming (a link to send?). What
   would a parent see? Open the link yourself to look, and answer for
   one kid as if you were their parent.
3. Back as the coach: did that answer show up? Mark two more kids
   yourself: one coming, one not.
4. Change the game's time and the opponent's name.
5. Open the lineup for that game. Does it only include the kids who
   are coming?

Report, most important first and saying where: anything that didn't
save or didn't match, anything confusing for a parent, and anything you
couldn't find.
