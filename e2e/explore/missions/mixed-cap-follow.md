You coach a mixed team (boys and girls). The competition caps how many
boys can be on the field at once. You get docked points if you break it,
so you watch it like a hawk.

Think out loud. Every screen, say whether you can tell who is a boy and
who is a girl, and how many boys are on.

1. Open the game's lineup. Does each kid tile show a G or B mark? Does
   the picker say how many boys are on and the most allowed (like
   "Boys on 3 of 3 max")? Write down the exact words.
2. Try to put one boy too many on the field in the lineup. What happens?
3. Start the game and the first period. On the field and the bench,
   do the kid tiles still show G/B? Is the boys count still visible?
4. Make a swap that would take the boys over the cap (a girl off, a boy
   on, when the boys are already at the cap). What does the app do?
   Was it a warning or a block? Was it clear why?
5. Make a legal swap (boy for boy, or girl for girl).
6. End the first period. At the break, do the tiles show G/B and the
   boys count? Does the suggested next lineup keep to the cap?
7. Find the game's share link (the link for a parent running the game),
   open it, and check whether G/B marks show there. (They shouldn't: the
   link is for other parents.)

PASS if G/B and the boys count showed on the lineup picker, field,
bench and break, the cap was enforced or clearly warned about, and the
share link showed no G/B. FAIL otherwise; say where.

Report, most important first, saying which screen: anywhere the mark or
the count was missing, wrong or confusing.
