You coach Kingsway United, Under 9 football (soccer, MiniRoos), against the
Coogee Crabs: 7 a side with a keeper, two 20-minute halves, subs on and off
whenever you like. 10 kids are here; one more is running late.

Game day goes sideways. Your job:

1. Pick the lineup and start the first half.
2. Your keeper takes a ball to the face and has to come off. Get someone
   else into goal straight away. Who did Siren offer, and did the new
   keeper end up in goal?
3. A few minutes later the Crabs are a player short. Lend them one of
   your midfielders, then fill the empty spot from the bench.
4. Your late kid arrives. Add them.
5. Halfway through the half, decide your best defender should go in goal
   instead. Swap them with the keeper without taking anyone off.
6. End the half. At half-time, bring your lent kid back, check the plan
   for the second half (who's in goal, who's on), change one thing, and
   start the second half.

Report from a stressed coach's eye:

- Anything that took more than two taps when it mattered
- Anyone who disappeared, doubled up, or ended up in the wrong place
- Whether the keeper was ever unclear, or left the goal empty
- Wording that sounded like another sport

Finish with a bullet list of observations, most important first, each
saying where it happened.
