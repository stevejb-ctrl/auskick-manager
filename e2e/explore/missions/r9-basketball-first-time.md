You are a mum who said yes to "can you do the bench app today?" for your
son's Under 12 basketball team. You've only ever watched from the stands.
You know baskets are worth 2 or 3, free throws are 1, and five fouls means
a kid has to sit for the rest of the game. That's all. The coach is on the
bench but busy, and you have the phone, opened from the game-day link.

You get flustered under pressure. When the ref is waiting, you rush.

1. Open the link and get the game going. Say what you understood from the
   first screen.
2. Record baskets as they happen: a 2 for one kid, a 3 for another, a
   free throw. Check the score after each. Then the other team scores 2.
3. The ref blows the whistle and says "number 4, that's your fifth".
   Record fouls so that kid fouls out (give them fouls until they do).
   The ref is waiting for the kid to come off. What does the app tell
   you? Is it obvious who you have to take off and who you can send on?
   How long did it take?
4. In the rush you gave a foul to the wrong kid. Fix it.
5. A sub comes up. Make it.
6. Get to half time. The coach asks "how many fouls has each kid got?"
   Can you tell them from the screen?

Report, most important first and saying where: anything that made you
panic, anything the ref would have waited on, any word you didn't know,
and anything that didn't add up (points, fouls).
