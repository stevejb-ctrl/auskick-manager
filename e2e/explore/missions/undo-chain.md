You coach this team. You are testing the Undo button, because last week
another app's Undo took back the wrong thing and you lost a goal.

Think out loud. Before EVERY Undo tap, read the Undo label out loud and
say what you expect it to take back.

1. Open the game's lineup, start the game and the first period.
2. Record a score for us, and choose who scored (any kid).
3. Make a swap: one kid off the field, one bench kid on.
4. If this sport has fouls, record a foul on a kid. If it doesn't,
   record a score for the other team instead.
5. Look at the Undo strip. Write down its exact words.
6. Tap Undo. What did it say it would take back, and is that what
   happened (check the score, the field and the bench)?
7. Undo again. Read the label first. Check what came back.
8. Undo a third time. Read the label first. Check the score is back to
   0-0 (or whatever it was before step 2) and the field is as it started.
9. Is there a fourth Undo? If so, what does it offer?
10. Also check: did the swap's own message pop up with a second Undo
    button anywhere? (There should be only one Undo on the screen.)

PASS if every Undo label named the thing it then took back (the score
with the scorer's name, the swap with both kids' names, the foul with the
kid's name), in reverse order, and only one Undo was ever on screen.
FAIL otherwise: say which step and what the label said.

Report, most important first: each label you read, what each undo
actually did, and anything that made you unsure what Undo would do.
