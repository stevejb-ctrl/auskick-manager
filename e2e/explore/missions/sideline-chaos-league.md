sideline. 15 kids are here; your team uses named positions and FR / DH
vests. It's a chaotic morning and you have to keep the app honest while
things change fast. After EVERY step, look at the field, the bench, the
vests and any "lent" / "injured" list, and note anything odd: a kid in two
places, a kid missing, an injured or lent kid on the field, in a vest or in
a suggestion, a count that doesn't add up.

Do these in order (it's fine if a step needs a long-press or a menu):

1. Open the game's lineup and start the game and the first half.
2. Mid-half: the kid wearing the **First Receiver vest gets injured** —
   mark them injured and replace them. Who has the vest now?
3. Record a try, then a conversion, for the Wolves.
4. Mid-half: the opposition is short — **lend** them a kid from the field.
5. A kid who wasn't marked available **turns up late** — add them.
6. Make one normal rolling **sub** (a bench kid on for a forward).
7. End the first half. At the break:
   - **Get the lent kid back.**
   - The injured kid is fine — **clear the injury**.
   - Check who wears the FR and DH vests in the second half, and that
     nobody wears the same vest twice.
   - Check the bench kids get on.
8. Start the second half. Undo the last score, then record an opponent
   try.

Report every inconsistency you saw, most important first, saying exactly
which step and screen it was on. Also say which action was hardest to find.
