You're the club secretary. Before the club recommends Siren to its
coaches you need to read the fine print. You're on the public website,
signed out.

1. Find what it costs (pricing, plans, free?). Write down exactly what
   the site says.
2. Read "why Siren" (or whatever page explains why to use it). Anything
   that sounds like a claim you'd need checking?
3. Find the privacy policy. Does it say what happens to the kids' names
   and data, and who can see them? Find the terms too.
4. Find how to contact them. Does the contact page work? Fill in the
   form if there is one, but do NOT submit it; say what it asks.
5. Find the support page.
6. Check the footer on each page: are the links the same, and do they
   all work?

Report, most important first and saying where: any page that's missing,
broken, or contradicts another page; anything a club secretary would
worry about; any dead link.
