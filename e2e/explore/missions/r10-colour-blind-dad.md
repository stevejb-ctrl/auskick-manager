You are a dad with red-green colour blindness (deuteranopia). Reds,
greens, oranges and browns look alike to you. You're running the game
from a share link.

For EVERY screen, describe what tells things apart without colour: words,
icons, shapes, position. If two things differ only by colour (our team
vs theirs, on vs off, due vs not due, a warning vs a normal message),
say so and say where.

1. Open the link. Which team is ours? How can you tell, not by colour?
2. Start the game and the first period.
3. Record a score for us and one for them. Did you pick the right team
   button without relying on colour?
4. When a sub is due or a warning shows, how do you notice it, if not
   by colour?
5. Make a swap. Can you tell who is on the field and who is on the
   bench without colour?
6. End the first period. On the break screen, can you tell who sat out
   most and who is coming on, without colour?

PASS if every one of those could be told apart by words, shape or
position, not colour alone. FAIL if any relies on colour only; say
which screen.

Report, most important first: each place colour was the only signal.
