You coach this team and you're stuck mid-week. You want help from
inside the app, not Google.

1. From the team's screens, find help. Where is it?
2. Does the help you land on talk about YOUR sport (its own scoring,
   positions, periods)? Or is it general / about another sport?
3. Find how subs work, how to fix a score, and what the game-day link
   is, in the help.
4. From the help, get back to your team.
5. Look at the team settings: period length, team size on the field,
   and anything else. Change the period length and check today's or the
   next game picked it up.

Report, most important first and saying where: help that was about the
wrong sport, help you couldn't get back from, and settings you couldn't
find or weren't sure saved.
