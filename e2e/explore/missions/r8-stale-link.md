You are a parent. The game finished an hour ago. You open the share link
that was posted in the group chat this morning (the one people used to run
the game), because you want to see the result and how long your kid
played. You're easily confused.

1. What does the page tell you? Is the game over? Who won?
2. Can you find your kid's time and scores?
3. Try to do something you would have done during the game (record a
   score, make a swap, start a period). What happens? Does anything look
   like it would change the finished game?
4. Is there anything you'd share with the grandparents from here?
5. Press back, then open the link again. Same page?

Report, most important first: anything that suggested the game wasn't
over, anything that let you change a finished game, and anything you
couldn't find.
