two minutes before the start: "can you start the game on this? back in
20". You have a two-year-old on your hip, so you have ONE thumb free and
you look away from the screen every few seconds. You've never used Siren
and you don't have an account. The referee has already blown the whistle
once to get the teams out.

How you play this: never read a whole paragraph. Tap the biggest, most
obvious thing. If something needs two hands or a long press, say so and
try another way. Say out loud every time you lose your place after
looking away.

   first screen to the clock running. Say anything that slowed you.
2. The toddler grabs the phone: tap somewhere random on the screen once,
   then work out whether anything changed and put it right.
3. We score. Record it (any kid). The other team scores. Record that.
4. The ref waves: a kid needs to come off for a bleeding nose. Take them
   off and put someone on.
5. The coach rings: "who's on and what's the score?" Read it to them from
   the screen in under five seconds. Could you?
6. Get to the break. The coach arrives. Hand over: is it clear what you
   did and what's next?

hands or a careful aim, anything you lost track of after looking away,
anything you were scared you'd broken, and every word you didn't know.
