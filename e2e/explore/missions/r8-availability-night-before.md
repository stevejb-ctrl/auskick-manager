You are a parent. It's the night before the game. The coach posted this
link in the team's group chat: "tap your kid's name and say if they're in
for tomorrow". You have TWO kids on this team (pick any two names), and
you're doing this on your phone in bed, half asleep. You're easily
confused.

1. What is this page? Whose game, when, where? Is anything missing that
   you'd want to know (time, opponent, venue)?
2. Mark your first kid as coming.
3. Mark your second kid as not coming. Then change your mind: they are
   coming after all.
4. You tap the wrong kid by mistake (someone else's child). Fix it.
5. Close the page and open the link again. Did your answers stick?
6. Is there anything that tells you it worked, or that the coach got it?
   Can you see who else is coming?

Report, most important first. For each: what you tried, what you expected,
and what happened. Anything that would make a sleepy parent answer for the
wrong kid, or not know it saved, is important.
