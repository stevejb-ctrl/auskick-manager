It's a messy Saturday. You coach this team; today's game is in Siren.
Everything goes wrong at once, and you must keep the game right.

1. Start the game from the lineup.
2. In the first minutes: a kid who wasn't marked available turns up.
   Add them.
3. A kid on the field gets hurt. Mark them injured. Who came on? Was the
   right kind of player chosen (positions, keeper, the mix)?
4. The other team is short: lend them one of your field kids. Who
   replaced them on your side?
5. Score twice for us and once for them, and once by mistake for the
   wrong team; fix that one.
6. At the break: bring the lent kid back, and the hurt kid is fine now:
   put them back in. Start the next period. Is everyone where you
   expected?

Report, most important first: anything that put a kid in two places,
lost a kid, chose a replacement you'd never pick, or changed the lineup
without telling you.
