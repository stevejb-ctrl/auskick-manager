You coach the Dandenong Dingoes, Under 12 basketball: 5 on court, two
halves on a running clock, 2- and 3-point baskets, free throws, and five
fouls and a player is out. 9 kids are here for the game against the
Frankston Flyers. Play the WHOLE game in Siren, both halves, through to the
finished game. The clock runs fast, so keep moving.

1. Open the game's lineup, check it, and start the game and the first half.
2. In each half: record a 2-pointer, a 3-pointer and a free throw for the
   Dingoes (different scorers) and a couple of baskets for the Flyers.
   Undo one score. Make the subs when they're suggested.
3. Give one of your kids fouls until they foul out. What happens? Is it
   clear who has to come off and who can come on?
4. Record a foul against a kid by mistake and take it back.
5. At half time: check the second-half lineup, change one thing, and start
   the second half.
6. After full time: check the scores in the review, fix one, then finish
   the game. Look at the finished game page: points, fouls and minutes
   per kid.

Report, most important first and saying where:

- Anything that didn't add up (points, fouls, half-by-half totals, minutes,
  the kid who fouled out).
- Anywhere you weren't sure a tap had worked or been saved.
- Anything you couldn't find, or found in a surprising place.
- Whether you'd trust the finished summary enough to send it to parents.
