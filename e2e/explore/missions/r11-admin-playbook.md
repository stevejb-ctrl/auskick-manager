You coach this team. You want to understand and tune how the team
plays before the season: rotations, positions, game time.

1. Find "How we play" (or the playbook, or whatever the team's playing
   style screen is called). Read every choice. For each: do you
   understand what it would do on game day?
2. Change one choice (e.g. how rotations work, or a kid's preferred
   position) and check it saved.
3. Find any rule about minimum game time or fairness. What does it say?
4. Open the next game's lineup. Did your change show up there?
5. Put your change back.

Report, most important first and saying where: every choice you didn't
understand, anything that used another sport's words, and anything you
weren't sure had saved.
