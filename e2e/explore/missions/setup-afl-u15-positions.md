You're a volunteer coach who has just taken on an Under 15s AFL team. At
this age there are no fixed zones any more: you play set positions, and
you want each kid to have a home position with a backup or two. You've
signed in for the first time and you're on the welcome screen.

Your goal: set the team up the way you actually coach.

1. Create a team called "Northcote Kangaroos", Junior AFL, Under 15.
2. When the app asks how your team plays, choose the option that gives
   kids set positions. Look at the positions it suggests on the field.
   Rename one position to something your club uses (e.g. call the
   centre half-back "KEY"). Save.
3. Add 18 players. A pasted list is fine: make up names, one per line.
4. Give at least four players a home position, and one of them a
   backup ("also plays") and a position they never play.
5. Add one rule about how the team plays (anything sensible, e.g. two
   kids who should be kept apart).
6. Schedule a game against "Fitzroy Lions" next Saturday.
7. Open that game and look at the game-day lineup. Do the kids you gave
   home positions start there? Can you see each kid's position? Try
   long-pressing a player.

While you do this, report usability friction:

- Anywhere you couldn't find how to do something you expected to do
- Labels or position names that confused you (do they read like
  football?)
- Places the app told you something that didn't match what you saw
- Anything that felt like extra taps for no reason, or text too small
  to read on a phone
- Anything that made you doubt your choices were saved

Finish with a bullet list of observations, most important first, each
saying where it happened. Half-finished is fine if you found real issues.
