It's Saturday. You coach the Randwick Rams, Under 12 rugby union, playing
the Coogee Crabs soon. It's 15 a side in two 25-minute halves, and your
team plays named positions (numbered as on the jersey: props 1 and 3, the
hooker 2, 2nd row 4 and 5, flankers 6 and 7, number 8, halfback 9,
five-eighth 10, wings 11 and 14, centres 12 and 13, fullback 15). Your
halfback kicks for goal. 19 kids are here.

Your job:

1. Open the game's lineup. Does it look like a 15-a-side union team, with
   all fifteen positions on the field? Is anything there that belongs to
   rugby league or AFL (vests, kickoff or kicking rotations, "unbroken"
   rules, league position names like a lock at 13)?
2. Start the game and the first half.
3. Record a try for the Rams, then a conversion by your halfback.
4. Record a penalty goal by the halfback and a drop goal by your
   five-eighth.
5. Record a try and a drop goal for the Crabs.
6. Check the score: Rams 5 + 2 + 3 + 3 = 13, Crabs 5 + 3 = 8. Then undo
   the Crabs' drop goal and check again.
7. End the first half and check the score at the break.

Report usability friction from a union coach's eye:

- Was it obvious where to record a penalty goal or a drop goal?
- Did the points match union's (try 5, conversion 2, goals 3)?
- Anything that looked like rugby league or AFL
- Taps that felt slow or risky on the sideline

Finish with a bullet list of observations, most important first, each
saying where it happened. Half-finished is fine if you found real issues.
