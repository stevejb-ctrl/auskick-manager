It's Saturday. You coach the Brunswick Bears, an Under 15 AFL team that
plays set positions. The team is set up in Siren with 20 kids and a game
against Sandringham Sharks soon, but you haven't given any kids
positions yet. You've got ten minutes before the bounce.

Your job:

1. Give five kids their home positions quickly. Do it whichever way
   seems fastest: from the squad, or straight from the game's lineup.
   Make one of them your full forward and one your full back.
2. Open the game's lineup. Do those five start in their positions? Can
   you tell every kid's position on the field? Swap two kids and check
   their positions follow.
3. Start the game and the first quarter. Your full forward kicks a goal:
   record it.
4. Decide your full back should stay in the back line all game, not
   just stay on. Find how to do that.
5. At the quarter break, check the suggested changes. Does the bench
   swap like-for-like (a back for a back)?

Report usability friction:

- Anything that made setting positions slow under time pressure
- Position names or labels you'd misread at a glance
- Anything about the lineup that didn't match the positions you set
- Anything you expected to be able to do but couldn't find

Finish with a bullet list of observations, most important first, each
saying where it happened. Half-finished is fine if you found real issues.
